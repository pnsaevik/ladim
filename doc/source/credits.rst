Credits
=======

Ladim was originally developed as a Fortran program by Bjørn Ådlandsvik in 1994 to support
research projects and advisory activities on marine dispersal at the
`Institute of Marine Research (IMR) <https://www.hi.no>`_. Bjørn Ådlandsvik
maintained responsibility for the codebase until 2023, at which point it had
been rewritten as a Python package. Today, the package is maintained by the
research group for coastal oceanography at the IMR.
